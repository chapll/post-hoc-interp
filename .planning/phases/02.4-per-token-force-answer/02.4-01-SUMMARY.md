## Decisions Made
- **Inline probing loop over probe_trace() call** -- probe_trace() processes all positions in one call with no hook for periodic empty_cache(). Inlining the loop from probe_trace() gives control over periodic memory cleanup every 100 probes, which is important for 824 iterations.
- **Direct trace loading by path** -- load_traces_for_experiment() does dataset-balanced random selection across multiple traces. For one specific known trace, loading directly by constructed path is simpler and more explicit.
- **Transition characterization metrics** -- computed: correct-to-incorrect transitions, incorrect-to-correct transitions, longest correct/incorrect runs, first sustained correct position (k=5), middle-third probability variance. These quantify the transition shape that the 20-point grid could not resolve.
