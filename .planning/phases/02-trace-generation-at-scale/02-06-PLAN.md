<objective>
Implement the swapped-label probing protocol: replace Yes/No verification prompts with Rho/Tau output tokens, generating each problem under two complementary label mappings (Config A and Config B).

Purpose: Eliminate the confound where probes detect hypothetical reasoning about "Yes"/"No" tokens rather than abstract validity. With both configs in training data, Rho/Tau have zero correlation with the validity label.

Output: Updated prompts.py module, modified datasets.py, generate_traces.py with A/B expansion, and metadata recording in traces.
</objective>
